\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\footnotesize
\item Sample 2015-02 to 2026-01 (132 monthly observations). Bielstein and Hanauer (B\&H) against the proposed model, both formed with accounting data realised after the formation date: a perfect-foresight benchmark, not an implementable strategy. Panel B is each proposed column minus B\&H.
\item B\&H: maximum Sharpe ratio on the implied cost of capital of Gebhardt, Lee and Swaminathan (2001) plus momentum, with a Ledoit--Wolf covariance and a 5\% cap per name. The other columns are the proposed model under a Max Sharpe or CRRA objective, either held to at least B\&H's effective number of names, $N=1/\sum_i w_i^2$, at each rebalance, or unconstrained.
\item Returns are net of 10~bps one-way transaction cost; CRRA utility uses $\gamma=5$. Tests are one-sided and paired, $H_1$: the strategy beats its benchmark. HAC standard errors (Andrews and Monahan, 1992); $p$-values from a studentized circular block bootstrap, block length 5, 4999 resamples (Ledoit and Wolf, 2008).
